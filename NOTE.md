# Am I exam-ready? A short note
*Astha Purohit*

## How would I know if the score is right?

The app says "Ready" when it thinks a learner will pass. To know if that's true, I would check it against real exam results.

- After learners take the real exam, I would compare their exam result with what the app told them. If most "Ready" learners pass, the score works. If many of them fail, the app is too generous, and I would make "Ready" harder to get.
- Exam results take time, so I would also ask teachers: "Do you agree with what the app said about this learner?"
- I would look at each question. If good learners get a question wrong and weak learners get it right, it's a bad question and I would replace it.

The worst mistake is telling someone they're ready when they're not, because they lose their exam fee. So I would watch that most closely.

## What would I ask 5 learners before building version 2?

1. How did you decide when to book your exam?
2. Which part of the exam scared you most?
3. If the app said "Almost there", would you wait, or book anyway?
4. Was it easy to choose "sure" or "guessing"?
5. If you already took the exam, did the app's result match your real score?
